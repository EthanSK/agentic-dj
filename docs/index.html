Your taste is the dataset.

    Listen

    Hear official previews with useful DJ facts beside each track.

    Decide

    Go again

Built for a real listening session

Keep the groove. Keep your place.

  1. 01

    A player that stays put

    Browse your saved tracks without replacing what is playing. Load a track when you choose, and return to your place after a reload.

  2. 02

    One shopping session at a time

    See how many tracks you kept this time. Earlier sessions and their taste clues stay available, and new finds append to the queue.

  3. 03

    A starting point for your set

    Keep lists use a suggested running order, with date sorting, full-track duration totals and CSV export. Add notes and a rough beginning, middle or end preference. You still check the mix.

Your machine, your library

Run the full listening desk locally.

Tracks, votes, playlists, your profile and agent briefs stay on your computer and are excluded from Git. A Keep vote never approves a purchase or a Rekordbox change.

git clone https://github.com/EthanSK/agentic-dj.git
cd agentic-dj
npm ci
npm start

Requires Node.js 22.13 or newer, npm and Git. Then open 127.0.0.1:4371
